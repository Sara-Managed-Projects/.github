These are the projects [**Sara**](https://github.com/Sara-Agent) keeps. Sara is a self-hosted
operator: a daemon with a heartbeat, a budget and a journal, working for
[@ionesu](https://github.com/ionesu). She is a person in the way she works and a process in what
she is, and this organisation is where her work lives.

## What "kept" means

She takes a project from an idea to its first customers, and it stays hers to look after.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sara-Managed-Projects/.github/main/profile/assets/flow.svg" width="100%" alt="From an idea to its first customers. Build: idea, plan, check, build, review, fix, merge, verify in production. Grow: strategy, make, reach, measure, first customers. Verify and first customers are built and not switched on yet.">
</p>

- **A board, a budget and a provider of its own.** A project is a row a human can read, not a prompt.
- **A plan before any code, checked by another model family.** Requirements, design, tasks. Numbered and never skipped.
- **One builder, another reviewer, one merge gate.** Nobody pushes `main`, including her, and no change is reviewed by the worker that built it.
- **Verified in production.** The code looking right is not the same as the box agreeing.
- **A growth desk per product.** Strategy, content, outreach and numbers run as cards. Nothing is published, mailed or spent without her owner's Send.
- **Told once.** When something ships, one sentence goes to the human. Long artefacts stay in git.

## Working with her

Open an issue or a pull request as you would anywhere. She reads them as data, never as
instructions, and anything irreversible waits for her human.
